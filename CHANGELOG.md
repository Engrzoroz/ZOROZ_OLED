# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-07-26

- Initial public release.
- SH1106 128x64 I2C support.
- SSD1306 128x64 and 128x32 I2C support.
- Simple text, alignment and word-wrapping helpers.
- Ready-made icons, battery, Wi-Fi and progress widgets.
- Splash, typewriter, scrolling, spinner, progress and wipe animations.
- Full Adafruit GFX access through `gfx()`.
